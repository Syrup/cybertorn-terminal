# CyberTorn Terminal: AI Asset Generation Guide

Use these prompts to generate visual assets that match the **Anti-Vibe / CyberTorn** aesthetic: high-density, technical, utilitarian, and monochromatic with minimal neon accents.

## 1. Primary Branding (Logo/Icon)
**Tool:** Midjourney / DALL-E 3
**Prompt:**
> A minimalist vector logo for a technical terminal, single thick white line forming a stylized "C" and "T" integrated into a square circuit pattern, flat design, no gradients, black background, high contrast, engineering schematic style, 8-bit industrial aesthetic.

## 2. Background Textures (Subtle)
**Tool:** Midjourney (--tile for seamless)
**Prompt:**
> Seamless pattern of a vintage computer CRT phosphor grid, ultra-dark charcoal gray, faint scanning lines, microscopic electronic traces, technical blueprint texture, low opacity, no glows, flat matte finish.

## 3. Section Hero/Schematics
**Tool:** Flux.1 / Midjourney
**Prompt:**
> Technical wireframe drawing of a futuristic city grid, top-down tactical view, CAD software style, white lines on deep black, no shading, isometric projection, labels with monospaced font, industrial UI element, clean mechanical lines.

## 4. Item Placeholders (Equipment)
**Tool:** DALL-E 3
**Prompt:**
> Raw UI screenshot of a 16-bit inventory item, a tactical combat knife, monochromatic green on black, pixel art style, dithered shading, transparent background, isolated, technical readout overlay.

---

## Visual Rules for AI Tools:
- **Avoid:** "Cinematic lighting", "3D render", "Glassmorphism", "Vibrant colors".
- **Prefer:** "Flat design", "Schematic", "Monochromatic", "High density", "Technical drawing", "IBM Plex style".
- **Color Palette:** `#000000` (Background), `#FFFFFF` (Primary), `#00FF41` (Optional Matrix Green).
